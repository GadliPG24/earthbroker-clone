About EarthBroker

Your Sourcing Solution for New & Used Equipment and Machinery

Home / About

The Earthbroker was founded in 2004 and is an A4 magazine published monthly. 5,400 copies are sent to all the mines, quarries and all companies dealing with earthmoving and construction in South Africa, into Africa and overseas.

The publication is your sourcing solution for new and used earthmoving machines and parts. We also have a digital version of each issue which is uploaded monthly with the latest edition of the magazine.

Our advertisers also enjoy free online exposure on our digital magazine and we also have online classifieds where machinery and parts can be uploaded for a nominal fee.

  • + Digital magazine version uploaded monthly
  • + Free online exposure for advertisers
  • + Online classifieds platform

  • + Coverage across South Africa and into Africa
