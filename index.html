About EarthBroker

The Earthbroker was founded in 2004 and is an A4 magazine published monthly. 5,400 copies are sent to all mines, quarries and all companies dealing with earthmoving and construction in South Africa, into Africa and overseas.

The publication is your sourcing solution for new and used earthmoving machines and parts. We also have a digital version of each issue which is uploaded monthly.
